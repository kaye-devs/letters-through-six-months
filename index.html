Letters Through
Six Months

Six months may feel like a long time, but I'll fill every day with little reminders of how much I love you.

Hi, Love.

Before you go, I want you to know this.

Today is September 04, 2026, and here I am, creating this little website for us. I was working on my portfolio through HTML when I suddenly thought, why not create something for us, too? So, here it is—a little corner of the internet that belongs to us.

For now, this website is offline, but someday, when you look through it, I hope you can feel how much love went into every little part of it. I hope you appreciate it, even just a little, because I made it with you in mind.

This will be the journal I've been telling you about. I want to fill these pages with memories, photos, videos, little updates about my days, random thoughts, and letters for you while you're away. Maybe some entries will be long, some will be short, and some might simply say that I missed you a little extra that day.

Six months sounds like such a long time when we're used to having each other around.

There will be days when I miss you more than usual. Days when I wish I could simply hug you and stay there for a little while longer. Days when all I want is to hear your voice, tell you about my day, or have you beside me while we're doing absolutely nothing.

There might also be difficult days. Days when things feel heavy, days when I feel lonely, and days when I wish the six months would pass a little faster.

But even on those days, I want to remember why we're doing this.

You're going away to work hard for your dreams, and I am so proud of you for having the courage to take this step. I know it won't always be easy for you either. There will be tiring days, stressful days, and moments when you miss home and miss us.

So when those days come, please remember that you are loved.

You have someone here who believes in you, who is proud of you, and who will always be cheering for you from afar.

Before you go, I want you to be strong. Have faith in yourself, have faith in us, and most importantly, have faith in God. Always pray, especially when things become difficult. Ask Him for strength when you're tired, guidance when you're uncertain, protection wherever you go, and peace when your heart feels heavy.

Take care of yourself for me.

Eat properly. Get enough rest whenever you can. Don't push yourself too hard. Be careful wherever you go, and please remember that you don't always have to be strong on your own. If you ever feel tired, scared, or overwhelmed, it's okay to pause, breathe, pray, and remind yourself that you're not alone.

I may not be physically there beside you, but my heart will be with you through every step.

And while you're working hard toward your dreams, I'll be here too. I'll be cheering for you, supporting you, growing, learning, and healing myself. I'll keep building my own dreams while patiently waiting for the day when we can finally be together again.

I don't want these six months to only be about missing you.

I want them to be about growing.

Growing individually, growing together, becoming stronger, and learning how to love each other even when distance makes it difficult.

I want us to come out of these six months knowing that we made it through something that once felt so long and scary.

And when you finally come home, I hope we can look back at this little journal and laugh at the things I wrote, cry over the memories we made while apart, and realize that somehow, we made it through every single day.

Until then, this will be my way of keeping a little piece of us close.

A place where I can leave you pieces of my days.

A place where our memories can live.

A place where my words can reach you, even when I can't.

So, Love, wherever you are when you're reading this, please remember:

I love you.

I am proud of you.

I believe in you.

I believe in us.

And no matter how many kilometers are between us, you will always have a home in my heart.

Don't count the days because they feel long.

Count the memories.

Count the little victories.

Count every prayer.

We'll get through this.

One day at a time.
One memory at a time.
One letter at a time.

Until six months become six weeks,
six weeks become six days,
and six days become "Welcome home, Love."

I love you so much.

More than these words could ever explain.

Now go chase your dreams.

I'll be here, waiting, loving, praying, and cheering for you every step of the way.

Until we meet again, my love.

K♡
